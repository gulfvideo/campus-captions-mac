Scope: the campuscaptions.app marketing page. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: AV directors, worship production leads and accessibility staff at
colleges, churches and conference venues, arriving sceptical about what
captioning costs. Job: decide whether to request beta access. Action: the
waitlist form (name, email, org, role, kind, cadence, notes — field names are
fixed by the Pages Function). Constraints: no price may be stated, no
Faulkner branding, no invented proof, and the "automatic captions are not a
legal accommodation" statement stays prominent.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Captioning an event costs nothing to run, and the audio never has to
leave the building. This surface refuses the category-default landing
arrangement — headline over subcopy over a row of equal feature cards — which
is exactly what the previous version of this page was.

OWN-WORLD: Dark Booth. Pure black ground, Card Graphite panels, one Signal
Blue action per screen, status hues for machine state only. Monospaced
spec-sheet voice for every measured fact; system sans for prose. Fully round
pressables, soft-rectangle containers, no gradient except the caption stage
floor. Rules are hairlines; depth is lightness, not shadow.

STORY: A production lead reads the document they already evaluate gear with.
COST / HOUR $0.00 arrives as a measured spec, not a claim. REQUIRES lists
equipment they own; OUTPUTS lists the three places captions land. They learn
what is unfinished before they are asked for anything, then join the waitlist.

FIRST VIEWPORT: One rider card fills the viewport on black. Masthead row —
CAMPUS CAPTIONS left, REV. BETA right — over a hairline. Beneath it a bordered
spec row: label COST / HOUR small and tracked left, $0.00 set at the largest
type on the page, right. Then two hairline-ruled monospace columns, REQUIRES
and OUTPUTS. A closing rule carries BETA — NOT FOR SALE at left and the
primary action, lit Signal Blue, at right. No hero image, no split layout.

FORM: The Tech Rider — index 5 of seven ordered structures, dealt and locked
by the user over the dealt lead (The Invoice That Isn't) and The Meter.
seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the
finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its
provenance

## Memorable moment

The spec row: a cost figure set at display scale where a landing page would
put a headline, stated in the flat register of a measurement.

## Unresolved
