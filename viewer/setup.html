Welcome

Campus Captions listens to the room, turns speech into captions, and puts them on two screens: a wall view for the projector or LED wall, and a phone view the audience opens on their own devices.

This wizard picks the microphone, chooses how captions are produced, and ends with the links to hand out. It takes about two minutes.

Captions never start on their own. Even after setup, no transcript is produced until someone presses Go Live on the status page — so you can leave this running through rehearsal at no cost.

Which microphone?
